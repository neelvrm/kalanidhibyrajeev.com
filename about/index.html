Artist & author

Rajeev Verma

Rajeev Verma is an artist and author based in Dehradun. He creates mandalas, spiritual paintings, portraits and decorative artwork. His writing at rajeevverma.org includes Hindi short stories and reflections on relationships, gratitude, honesty and everyday life.

He works with dot patterns, geometric designs and spiritual themes, alongside oil paintings and portraits. Custom artwork can be discussed directly with him.

He founded कलाNidhi to bring his artwork, books and workshops together. At the gallery on Canal Road in Jakhan, visitors can browse the collection, ask about a custom piece or enquire about upcoming sessions.

Artist

Handmade mandalas, portraits, spiritual paintings and decorative artwork, with custom orders available.

Poetry, Hindi short stories and published books, including DAD: A Tribute to Every Father.

Small-group sessions in mandalas, dot art and painting for beginners, children and adults.
