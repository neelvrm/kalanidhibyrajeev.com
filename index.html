Meet the person
behind the patterns.

Rajeev Verma is an artist, author, and mentor whose work moves between intricate mandalas and everyday Hindi stories. कलाNidhi brings these interests together in his Dehradun studio.

Browse a painting, talk about a book, learn a technique. There is room here for both looking and making.
