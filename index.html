About the artist

Rajeev Verma

Rajeev Verma is an artist and author based in Dehradun. His work includes mandalas, spiritual paintings, portraits, poetry and Hindi short stories.

At कलाNidhi, you can view his artwork, browse his books, discuss a custom order or learn in a small-group workshop.
